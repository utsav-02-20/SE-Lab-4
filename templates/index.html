Software Engineering Lab Specifications & Requirements Mapping

(a) Actors in the System

  • Online Shopping Portal / Customer: Submits origin, destination, and payload specs; tracks real-time consignment progression; requests schedule modifications or order cancellations.
  • Courier Partner / Delivery Agent: Receives automated assignments; updates transit milestones (Picked Up, In Transit, Out for Delivery, Delivered); receives payouts and on-time SLA bonuses; incurs penalties for SLA breaches.
  • Logistics Dispatcher / System Admin: Manages courier fleet and locality coverage matrix; monitors fleet capacity; oversees SLA settlement notices; executes archival of historical records.

(b) Use Cases per Actor

  • UC01: Book Delivery (Customer/Portal) — Evaluates candidate couriers based on locality, cost, rating, and load. Produces Output 1 (Booking Confirmation).
  • UC02: Check Delivery Status (Customer/Agent) — Queries transit logs. Produces Output 2 (Delivery Status Report).
  • UC03: Cancel Booking (Customer/Agent) — Terminates delivery, releases courier workload. Produces Output 3 (Cancellation Confirmation).
  • UC04: Reschedule Delivery (Customer/Dispatcher) — Updates promised SLA window. Produces Output 4 (Updated Record after Rescheduling).
  • UC05: Calculate Rewards/Penalties (System) — Compares actual completion against promised SLA. Produces Output 5 (Notice of Reward/Penalty).
  • UC06: Query Courier Past Services (Courier/Dispatcher) — Produces Output 6 (List of Past Services for a Given Courier).
  • UC07: Archive Deliveries (Dispatcher) — Archives concluded consignments.

(c) Entity-Relationship Data Model

  • couriers: id, name, phone, email, vehicle_type, max_capacity_kg, serviceable_localities, base_fare, rate_per_kg, rating, is_active, current_active_orders, total_completed_orders, wallet_balance.
  • deliveries: id, tracking_id, sender_name, sender_phone, source_address, source_locality, receiver_name, receiver_phone, dest_address, dest_locality, package_weight_kg, package_type, service_type, assigned_courier_id (FK), shipping_cost, courier_payout, status, booked_at, promised_delivery_time, actual_delivery_time, rescheduled_count, is_archived.
  • delivery_logs: id, delivery_id (FK), status, remarks, timestamp.
  • reschedule_logs: id, delivery_id (FK), old_delivery_time, new_delivery_time, reason, rescheduled_at.
  • cancellation_records: id, delivery_id (FK), cancelled_by, reason, cancellation_fee, refund_status, cancelled_at.
  • rewards_penalties: id, delivery_id (FK), courier_id (FK), type, amount, reason, notice_text, created_at.

(d) Constraints & Business Rules Enforced

  • Locality & Payload Constraint: Courier must service source/destination localities and have vehicle capacity ≥ consignment weight.
  • Sequential Transit Pipeline: Milestones must progress in strict order: Booked → Picked Up → In Transit → Out for Delivery → Delivered. Skipping steps is prevented.
  • Status Immutability Rule: Consignments with terminal status (Delivered, Cancelled, or Expired) cannot be modified or rescheduled.
  • SLA Reward / Penalty Rule: Delivery on or before SLA deadline awards +₹100 bonus; deliveries completed past SLA deadline trigger -₹50 penalty.
  • Deadline Expiry & Forfeiture: Consignments exceeding the delivery deadline without completion can be expired with a -₹100 SLA failure penalty deducted from the courier.
  • Archive Integrity Rule: Only terminal status records (Delivered, Cancelled, Expired) are eligible for archival.
